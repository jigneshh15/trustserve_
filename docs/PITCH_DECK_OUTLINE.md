# Pitch Deck (10–12 slides)

1. TrustServe title, team and tagline
2. Customer problem and evidence (interviews required; do not invent)
3. Target segment and initial Ahmedabad pilot hypothesis
4. Solution and user journey
5. Product demo/screenshots
6. Market sizing methodology (sources and assumptions; avoid unsupported numbers)
7. Business model and pricing hypothesis
8. Competition and differentiation
9. Go-to-market pilot and metrics
10. Technology/cloud architecture and trust/safety
11. Financial assumptions, costs, funding ask and use of funds
12. Roadmap, risks, ask and team
